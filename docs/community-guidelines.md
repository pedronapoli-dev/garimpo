# Community guidelines

Garimpo is music curation made by people. A good recommendation tells someone who has never heard of an artist why they should listen. These guidelines are the house rules; reports enforce them.

## Recommend what is lesser-known

Garimpo exists to surface music that algorithms and charts tend to miss. There is no numeric threshold (rule 3). Use judgment:

- **Good fit:** a regional scene, an out-of-print record, a debut with a few thousand plays, a forgotten album by a known artist, an artist big in one country and unknown elsewhere.
- **Poor fit:** what is already on every popular playlist, or what most people in your circle already know.

When in doubt, say in your text why the artist or record is overlooked.

## Explain why to listen

The text is the recommendation. 80 characters is the minimum, not the goal.

- Say what it sounds like and what makes it special: an arrangement, a voice, a moment in a track.
- Give a starting point for artists: an album or a track to begin with.
- Write in your own words. Do not paste press releases, reviews or lyrics.

## Tag honestly

Use 1 to 5 genres that describe the music, not the mood of the day. Reuse existing tags when they fit, so people browsing a genre find your recommendation.

## Be respectful

- Recommend music, not people to dislike. No hate, harassment or personal attacks.
- One recommendation per artist and title: if it already exists, vote for it instead of posting it again (rule 7).
- Do not use Garimpo to promote your own releases without saying so in the text.

## Reports

Report a recommendation when it breaks these guidelines, and say why. Each person can report a recommendation once. After **3 reports** it is hidden from the feed until a maintainer reviews it (rule 6). Reports are for guideline violations, not for music you dislike; for that, simply do not vote.

## Invites

Sign-up is invite-only (rule 1). Invite people who will recommend with care. Each code works once.
